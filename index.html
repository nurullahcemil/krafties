The Hype Gap

Is it worth it, or just hyped? Hype against reality for 708 famous places in 14 destinations, with real prices and parking.

thehypegap.com ↗

Principles

01Nobody pays for a verdict

No paid placements and no affiliate links on our ranking sites. A score only moves with evidence.

02Every claim shows its source

Prices, fees and facts link to where they came from, with the date we checked.

03Small, and kept current

Fewer products, maintained properly, rather than many left to go stale.

Design principles

01One question, answered well

Each product exists to answer one thing: is it worth it, is it legit, what’s on near me, which coffee, where a word comes from. Anything that doesn’t help answer it comes out.

02The answer first

The verdict sits at the top. The working, the sources and the detail are one tap below it, never in the way.

03Plain words, real numbers

Short sentences and the actual price, time or count, never “affordable” or “around”. If we don’t know something, we say so.

04Respect the person

No fake scarcity, no pre-ticked boxes and no guilt to get you to stay. Motion is there to explain something, and it stops if you’ve asked for less.

05Phone first, for everyone

Built for a phone on a slow connection, with readable contrast and big tap targets, and it works with a keyboard and a screen reader.
