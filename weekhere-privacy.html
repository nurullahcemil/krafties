01Who runs WeekHere

WeekHere (the WeekHere iPhone app and weekhere.com) is a Krafties product, published by Nurullah Gokdogan. KraftStories, the craft studio we also run, has workshops among the listings; on the website they are marked “Featured · run by WeekHere”. Questions and deletion requests: hello@weekhere.com.

02What the app sends to us

There is no sign-in and no account. Unlike our other apps, WeekHere talks to a server so it can send alerts. These are the only things it sends:

  • A random install id. Made on your phone the first time the app opens. It is not your advertising id or phone number.
  • A push token, only if you allow notifications. Apple issues it; it is the address an alert is delivered to.
  • Your city, its time zone, and the app version. So alerts are about the city you picked and never arrive at night.
  • Which alerts you chose and the kinds of events you like. The alert topics you switched on, the studios you follow, and the interest categories you picked.
  • Your name and email, only if you send a group request. Used to arrange the booking with the studio and reply to you.

03What stays on your phone

Your plans, saved events and kids’ ages never leave the phone. Location is used once, on the phone, if you tap “use my location” to pick the nearest city; it is never sent to us or stored. Adding an event to your calendar writes to your calendar and tells us nothing. The app has no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party trackers, and it does not use your advertising id.

04The website

weekhere.com counts visits with Google Analytics (pages viewed, rough location from the network, device type). If you join the Monday email, your address is held by Klaviyo, which sends it; every email has an unsubscribe link. If you submit an event or send a request, we keep what you typed and your email so we can reply.

05Who else handles it

Vercel (hosting), Neon (our database), Apple (delivers notifications), Klaviyo (the Monday email), Resend (replies and confirmations) and Google Analytics (website visits). Each gets only what its job needs. We do not sell or share your information. When you book a class, you book on the venue’s own site, under its own policy.

06Deleting it

In the app, You → Delete my data erases your alert settings and install id on our server and resets the app. For the email list, use the unsubscribe link. For anything else, write to hello@weekhere.com and we will delete it.

07Children

WeekHere lists family and kids’ events for parents. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

08Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it. Anything that would collect more is asked for in the app first.
